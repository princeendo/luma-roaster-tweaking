1. Juni Barista Mode + setup (once)

  • App: Grind Size = Medium, Basket = Cone (25–600 ml).
  • Set Coffee Dose = 15 g, Water Volume = 240 ml (Strength 1:16.0).
  • Bloom: 45 ml, 200 °F, 3.2 ml/s, Spiral, vib 5s / 5s, wait 30s.
  • Pour 2: 100 ml, 200 °F, 3.2 ml/s, Spiral, vib Off / Off, wait 10s.
  • Pour 3: 95 ml, 200 °F, 3.2 ml/s, Spiral, vib Off / Off, wait 10s. Check 240/240ml, tap Save.
  • Put filter in Cone, press Pre-Wet, dump carafe. Opus 2 = 6. Label 3 cups.

2. Brew each roast (repeat 3×)

  • Weigh 15.0 g beans on scale (record exact weight below).
  • Purge 1–2 beans or tap/brush grinder chute before grinding.
  • For cups 2 & 3: insert fresh filter, press Pre-Wet, dump carafe.
  • Grind, dump into filter, shake basket level, start Juni brew.
  • Pour into matching labeled mug. Let cool slightly before tasting.
