2. Brew each roast (repeat 3×)

  • For cups 2 & 3: insert fresh filter, press Pre-Wet, dump carafe.
  • Grind, dump into filter, shake basket level, start Juni brew.
  • Pour into matching labeled mug. Let cool slightly before tasting.
