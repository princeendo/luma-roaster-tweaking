Roast sheet: Costa Rica La Rosa (washed)

Roast 2026-11-10-01  |  CBR-120POT  |  First roast: find first crack, aim for light-medium (city+)

ProgramP01
Green beans90 g
Timer14 min
Expect 1st crack~8:30

Before

  • Read the rating label on the base (write below).
  • Range hood or open window. 30 cm clear all round.
  • No kettle or microwave on the same circuit.
  • Chaff bin empty. Intake screen clean.
  • Weigh the whole bag, then weigh out 90.0 g. Remove stones and broken beans.
  • Beans below MAX line. Lid, bin and chamber locked.
  • Stopwatch ready (count up).

Start

  1. Hold Power 3 s.
  2. Knob to P01, press Power.
  3. Knob to 14, press Power. Start the stopwatch on this press.
  4. Stay with the roaster the whole time.

Press Power to cool when:

  • 30 s after first crack ends
  • or 2:00 after first crack starts, if the end is unclear
  • or at once on second crack (fast, quiet crackle)
  • or at 13:00 (display 1:00), whatever has happened

Write the stopwatch time of each event
