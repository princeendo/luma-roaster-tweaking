Roast sheet: Costa Rica La Rosa (washed)

Roast 2026-11-11-01  |  CBR-120POT  |  Roast 2: two minutes longer than roast 1, aim for light-medium (city+)

ProgramP01
Green beans90 g
Timer16 min
Target roasted weight78–79 g

Before

  • Same place and outlet as roast 1. Roaster cold.
  • Range hood or open window. 30 cm clear all round.
  • No kettle or microwave on the same circuit.
  • Chaff bin empty. Intake screen clean.
  • Weigh out 90.0 g. Remove stones and broken beans.
  • Beans below MAX line. Lid, bin and chamber locked.
  • Stopwatch ready (count up).

Start

  1. Hold Power 3 s.
  2. Knob to P01, press Power.
  3. Knob to 16, press Power. Start the stopwatch on this press.
  4. Stay with the roaster the whole time.

Let the timer run out (16:00).
Press Power early only on:

  • second crack (fast, quiet crackle, like rice cereal in milk)
  • or heavy smoke
