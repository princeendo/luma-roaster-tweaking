1. Before (check each box)

  • Same place & outlet; nothing else large on circuit (~11.7 A).
  • Roaster fully cool (≥ 30 min since last run).
  • Range hood or open window; 30 cm clear all around.
  • Chaff bin empty; intake screen clean.
  • Weigh 70.0 g green beans (optional: weigh bag remainder).
  • Load beans below MAX line; lock lid, bin & chamber.
  • Count-up stopwatch ready; room temp noted below.

2. Start & stop rule

  1. Hold Power 3 s (shows P01).
  2. Confirm P01 → press Power once.
  3. Turn knob to 16 → press Power. Start stopwatch now.
  4. Stay with the roaster for the entire cycle.

Let the timer run out (16:00).
Press Power early only on:

  • second crack (fast, quiet crackle like rice cereal)
  • or heavy smoke, or an oily sheen on the beans
